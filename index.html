GAM3x0V3R Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the GAM3x0V3R Camera Kit application (“the App”) handles information when used to apply an augmented-reality mascot effect to a webcam feed for streaming.

Information the App accesses

The App requests access to the device camera so that the user’s live camera image can be used by Snap Camera Kit to render the selected augmented-reality Lens.

How camera information is used

The camera feed is used to create the live visual effect, including facial tracking and the display of the user’s eyes and mouth within the mascot. The App itself does not intentionally record, store, sell, or build a database of camera images, video, facial images, or biometric identifiers.

Data stored by the App

The App may store local configuration information on the user’s device, such as Lens display adjustments or other preferences. This local configuration is used only to restore the user’s chosen settings.

Snap Camera Kit

The App uses Snap Camera Kit, a service provided by Snap Inc., to render augmented-reality Lenses. Snap’s technology may process technical or usage information in accordance with Snap’s own privacy practices and applicable Camera Kit terms. Users should also review Snap’s Privacy Policy for information about Snap’s processing.

Sharing of personal information

The developer does not sell personal information. The App is not designed to transmit the user’s webcam recording to the developer or to provide the developer with stored copies of the user’s camera feed.

Children

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. It should only be used in accordance with the age requirements of the platforms and services on which it is run.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to minimise the collection and retention of personal information. Because no internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, users should also keep their device, browser and software up to date.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the App or its use of Camera Kit changes. The “Last updated” date above will be changed when the policy is revised.
